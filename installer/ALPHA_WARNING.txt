MYEVENT LABS DE-FEEDBACK v0.0.1 ALPHA
=====================================

This is ALPHA software and this installer is UNSIGNED.

Test under controlled conditions before any live event. This release is not
validated for show-critical use and does not replace correct loudspeaker
placement, gain structure, system tuning or a competent live-sound engineer.

De-Feedback v0.0.1 is a deterministic signal processor. It is not AI-powered
and makes no guaranteed gain-before-feedback improvement claim.

What this installer places on your system:

  * VST3 plugin:
      C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\MyEvent Labs De-Feedback.vst3
  * Standalone application:
      C:\Program Files\MyEvent Labs\De-Feedback\

Windows SmartScreen may warn that the publisher is unknown because the build is
not code-signed. Choose "More info" then "Run anyway" only if you trust this
build. Do not lower system security settings globally.

Full details: README.md and docs/ALPHA_LIMITATIONS.md in the installed folder
or in the repository:
https://github.com/MyEvent-Labs/de-feedback
